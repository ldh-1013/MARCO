# 마르코! — 심야 실내수영장 2.5D 맵 도안 AI 프롬프트

> 기획서 v0.3 §10.1 확정 좌표 기준. `marco-imagegen-prompts.md`(캐릭터·이펙트·UI)와 짝을 이루는 **맵 전용** 프롬프트 모음이다.

---

## 0. 먼저 — AI가 할 수 있는 것과 없는 것

**AI 이미지 생성은 좌표를 지키지 못한다.** "메인 풀 홀을 (18,15)~(38,27)에 배치"라고 아무리 정확히 써도 AI는 대략적인 분위기만 만든다. 그래서 두 가지를 **분리해서** 쓴다.

| 용도 | 도구 | 산출물 |
|---|---|---|
| **정확한 배치 도면** (그레이박스 제작 기준) | `docs/tools/iso_map.py` → SVG/PNG | 좌표 100% 정확. **이것이 정본** |
| **분위기·아트 방향 참고** | 아래 AI 프롬프트 | 좌표 부정확. 룩앤필 탐색용 |

**디자이너 작업 순서**: SVG 도면으로 구조를 잡고 → AI 컨셉으로 분위기를 정한 뒤 → ProBuilder로 블록아웃한다. **AI 이미지를 보고 치수를 재지 마라.**

## 1. 공통 스타일 프리픽스

`marco-imagegen-prompts.md`의 프리픽스와 다르다. 저쪽은 1인칭 인게임 뷰, 이쪽은 **위에서 내려다보는 설계 도안**이다.

```
Isometric 2.5D game level blueprint, 45-degree top-down angled view,
pure black background (#000000), thin glowing neon linework only,
architectural diagram aesthetic, no texture fills, wireframe-like room
volumes with faint translucent floors, high contrast, technical drawing
precision, dark horror game level design document
```

**공통 네거티브**:
```
photorealistic, warm lighting, cluttered props, first-person view,
top-down flat 2D, perspective distortion, people, furniture detail,
bright colors, painterly rendering
```

**색 규칙** (§16.2 — 벗어나지 말 것):

| 요소 | 색 |
|---|---|
| 배경 | `#000000` |
| 구역 윤곽선 | `#E8E8E8` |
| 밸브 · 상호작용 | `#FFB84D` (앰버) |
| 출구 · 도망자 동선 | `#35F0D0` (시안) |
| 출구 오브젝트(게임 내 문 발광, §16.2 — 10-03 등재) | `#40A669` (초록) — 문 형태 윤곽으로 구분, 시안과 색상 근접. ⚠ 이 도면의 출구 표기를 시안에서 초록으로 바꿀지는 미정 |
| 위험 표시 · L자 컷 | `#FF3B4D` (레드) |
| 2층 | 반투명 흰색, 점선 윤곽 |

## 2. 전체 맵 조감도

### 2.1 설계 도면 스타일 (팀 내부 기준용)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric cutaway blueprint of an abandoned indoor swimming pool facility,
L-shaped floor plan with the north-east corner cut away, two floors:
a ground floor with 11 rooms and a raised upper viewing deck floating
3.5 meters above showing only two small rooms, all rooms drawn as
low translucent boxes with glowing white edges, five amber (#FFB84D)
vertical marker pins rising from five separate rooms, two cyan (#35F0D0)
circular exit markers at opposite diagonal corners, a faint 2-meter grid
on the ground plane, technical annotation style, no labels needed
```

### 2.2 층 분리 뷰 (Exploded View)

2층 구조를 팀에 설명할 때 가장 효과적인 그림이다.

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Exploded isometric view of a two-story swimming pool facility level design,
the upper floor lifted high above the ground floor with thin vertical
connector lines showing where the two staircases align, ground floor
showing a large central pool hall and surrounding rooms, upper floor
showing only a long spectator deck and one small water tank room,
generous empty black space between the two layers, glowing white
wireframe edges, amber markers on both layers
```

### 2.3 분위기 컨셉 (아트 방향 탐색용)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric view of a pitch-black abandoned night swimming pool seen from
above, only the edges of the architecture glowing faintly as if revealed
by sound waves, a large dark pool of still water in the center reflecting
nothing, expanding circular ripple rings emanating from three separate
points in different rooms, oppressive emptiness, most of the level
swallowed in absolute darkness with only fragments visible
```

### 2.4 순환로 다이어그램 (§10.3)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric level flow diagram, four distinct closed-loop circulation paths
overlaid on a dim wireframe floor plan, each loop drawn as a continuous
glowing cyan (#35F0D0) line of different brightness, loops varying greatly
in size from a very large perimeter loop to a small tight service loop,
the underlying architecture faded to 20% opacity so the loops dominate,
movement-flow analysis diagram aesthetic
```

## 3. 밸브 구역 2.5D 디테일

밸브 5개가 각각 **다른 종류의 리스크**를 갖는다(§6.1). 그 차이가 그림에서 읽혀야 한다.

### 3.1 밸브 A — 기계실 (막다른 방, 출입구 1개)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric cutaway of a small dead-end machine room, glowing outlines of
large pipes and machinery boxes, exactly ONE doorway opening on the south
wall with no other exits anywhere, an amber (#FFB84D) valve wheel marker
at the center, the single exit emphasized with a subtle red (#FF3B4D)
outline to convey "no escape route", claustrophobic proportions
```

### 3.2 밸브 B — 메인 풀 깊은쪽 (수중 3.5m)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric cutaway of a large swimming pool hall with the water volume
shown as a translucent cyan (#35F0D0) prism at 10% opacity, the pool floor
sloping down to a deep end, an amber valve marker submerged at the deepest
point 3.5 meters below the surface, faint vertical dotted line from the
valve up through the water to the surface indicating the dive path,
the rest of the hall in near-total darkness
```

### 3.3 밸브 C — 물탱크실 (2층)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric view of an elevated water tank room on a second floor, a large
cylindrical tank outlined in glowing white lines, two separate metal
grating staircases leading up to it from below drawn in amber, the floor
of the room rendered as a translucent plane so the darkness of the ground
floor below is visible through it, sense of height and exposure
```

### 3.4 밸브 D — 약품창고 (통로 분기, 순찰 교차점)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric cutaway of a small chemical storage room with TWO separate
doorways on different walls, glowing outlines of shelving and drums,
an amber valve marker at the center, faint dotted movement lines passing
through both doorways to convey that this room sits on a patrol crossroads,
small and cramped but with escape options
```

### 3.5 밸브 E — 유아풀 존 (얕은 수중, 개방된 위치)

```
[공통 스타일 프리픽스]
Isometric cutaway of a shallow children's pool area, a wide low-depth
water volume in translucent cyan at 10% opacity, an amber valve marker
just below the shallow water surface, the surrounding area notably OPEN
with few walls and wide sightlines, contrasting with the enclosed rooms
elsewhere, exposed and visible from many directions
```

## 6. AI에 함께 넣을 참조 데이터

프롬프트만으로 구조가 안 잡히면, 아래 텍스트를 프롬프트 뒤에 붙여라. 좌표를 정확히 지키진 못하지만 **구역 개수와 상대적 위치 감각**은 개선된다.

```
Layout reference (52m x 40m, L-shape, north-east corner removed):
GROUND FLOOR — Lobby (north-west, main entrance exit),
  Chemical Storage (north-center, VALVE D), Laundry (north-east area),
  Locker Room (west), Main Pool Hall (center-east, VALVE B underwater),
  Lifeguard Office (west small), Children's Pool (south-west, VALVE E shallow water),
  Sauna (center-south small), Shower Room (center-south),
  Machine Room (far south-east, VALVE A, single entrance from south),
  Staff Corridor (long narrow strip along the south edge, leads to drain exit).
UPPER FLOOR (+3.5m) — Spectator Deck (above the pool hall),
  Water Tank Room (west, VALVE C).
Two exits at opposite diagonal corners: Main Entrance (north-west),
Drain Exit (south-east).
```

## 7. 금지 사항

| 금지 | 이유 |
|---|---|
| 팔레트 밖 색 | §16.2 팔레트 원칙 |
| 밝은 조명·앰비언트 | 이 게임은 완전 암전이다 |
| 가구·소품 디테일 | 실루엣 판독성 기준 20종 이내(§16.3) |
